MSME Application Roadmap & Guidelines

Step-by-step verified guide to applying for Odisha state incentives, single-window clearances, and central credit schemes.

1

Step 1: Obtain National Udyam Registration

Udyam registration is mandatory and zero-cost on the official Ministry of MSME portal. Requires Aadhaar and PAN.

  • Aadhaar number of proprietor/partner/director
  • Enterprise PAN Card & GSTIN
  • Bank account details & IFSC code
  • Zero administrative fee / 100% paperless
Open Official Udyam Portal ↗
2

Step 2: Register on Odisha GO-SWIFT Portal

Government of Odisha Single Window Portal (investodisha.gov.in) provides one-stop statutory clearances and land allotments within 15-30 days.

  • Combined Application Form (CAF) for 30+ statutory services
  • Time-bound statutory approvals under Odisha Right to Public Services Act
  • Pollution Control Board Consent to Establish (CTE)
  • IDCO industrial land and shed allotment
Visit GO-SWIFT Portal ↗
3

Step 3: Prepare Detailed Project Report (DPR)

Compile technical feasibility, financial viability, machinery quotations, and environmental compliance documentation.

  • Comprehensive project cost breakdown (Land, Building, Plant & Machinery)
  • Pro-forma invoices and quotations from registered equipment suppliers
  • 3-year projected cash-flow statement and debt-service coverage ratio
  • Pollution category categorization (Green, Orange, White)
4

Step 4: Bank In-Principle Sanction & Subsidy Claim

Submit DPR to financial institution for term loan under CGTMSE or state credit schemes; file subsidy claim with DIC.

  • Submit loan application to Scheduled Commercial Bank or OSFC
  • Obtain formal loan sanction letter and disbursement schedule
  • Lodge Capital Investment Subsidy claim with District Industries Centre (DIC)
  • Enroll under CGTMSE for collateral-free guarantee cover up to ₹5 Crore
5

Step 5: Inspection & Joint Verification

District Industries Centre (DIC) inspection committee conducts physical verification prior to subsidy disbursement.

  • Joint on-site physical verification by General Manager, DIC & financing bank
  • Verification of commercial production commencement date
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) credit into enterprise term-loan account
  • Annual compliance filing for ongoing interest subvention

Frequently Asked Questions on MSME Applications

Q: Is Udyam registration sufficient to claim Odisha MSME Policy 2022 subsidies?

Udyam registration is a mandatory statutory prerequisite. However, to claim capital investment subsidies or interest subventions under the Odisha MSMED Policy 2022, you must also obtain commercial production certificates and file claims directly with your local District Industries Centre (DIC).

Q: What is the maximum subsidy available under CM-SRIM in Odisha?

Under the Chief Minister's Special Relief for Industry & MSMEs (CM-SRIM), eligible enterprises receive 5% to 6% per annum interest subvention on term loans and working capital, with additional incentives for women-led and SC/ST enterprises.

Q: Can I apply for both Central and State Government schemes simultaneously?

Yes! An enterprise can leverage credit schemes like PMEGP or CGTMSE from the Central Government alongside state-level incentives like stamp duty exemptions and power tariff concessions under the Odisha MSMED Policy 2022, provided there is no duplication of the exact same subsidy component.
